# 2026-10-29 — Brazilian Portuguese (`pt`)

Why: Pavel asked for Brazilian Portuguese as a further language of moood.

- The world and the game «miniux» are in Brazilian Portuguese too: `"pt"` in their `languages`; `lang/pt.json`, its
  scenes' `scenes/<folder>/lang/pt.json` (Oficina, Convés), the game's `bespoke/lang/pt/strings.json`. Translated from
  the English, the Russian and the French read beside it; the memos for the author («Verificar: …») plain, «você».
- Texts the games show too are as those games' Portuguese has them (works-003, works-005 on their `pt` branches):
  Segurar, Os parafusos, O anel, Escutar, Mãos, «ficar»; the works as «Velas Escarlates», «A Queda da Casa de Usher»,
  «As Crisálidas». Cordame, A corda, Rumo, Acalmá-la are not texts of those games — chosen here.
- «Leave» here is «deixar» (leave the screw, leave the box shut), not Usher's «partir» (leave the house).
- The language's words are one word each, the same in every memo: placa (plate), barra, marca, anel, lasca (chip),
  entalhes (notches), brilho (glow), dedo fantasma, apresentação (showing), tentativa suave, truque, bifurcação,
  resultado, derrota; the error names as in the outcome line: errou, soltou cedo, no perigo, fora da linha, ficou para
  trás, lugar errado, cedo ou tarde demais, rápido demais, ao contrário, tranco.
- Checked with moood's `check` (its `pt` branch) — «all right — 300 texts in 4 scopes»; line breaks — `nl.py`, bad 0.
