# works-004: miniux, gathered (2026-10-28)

The owner regroups the old worlds and games into numbered repositories `works-NNN-…`; this is the fourth: miniux, the
playground of the mini-games' language — its world and its bespoke game.

## What and how

- **Gathered with their histories**, each from its `env/dev` (`git filter-repo --to-subdirectory-filter`, then
  `git merge --allow-unrelated-histories` into `main`): `moood-me/pavel--miniux` → `worlds/miniux/`,
  `pavel--game--miniux` → `games/miniux/`.
- **As moood has them now** (moood's `check` passes): world and game declare `"languages": ["en", "ru"]`; the game's
  `"world"` is the world's path (`../../worlds/miniux`); the old moood's `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md` removed; translations
  laid out by `check --write`.
- **Docs:** the miniux rounds' history from `moood-me/studio` (`docs/history/`). The language's own docs —
  `language.md`, `wishes.md`, `playground.md` — stay in moood (`docs/games/miniux/`). References to docs elsewhere are
  written `<owner>/<repo>:<path>`.
- The old repositories are left as they are.
